About

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Health Systems Management at The Max Stern Yezreel Valley College, Israel, where I teach courses in bioethics, health systems, and health policy. I hold a Ph.D. in Philosophy from Bar-Ilan University, where my doctoral dissertation examined The Right to Parenthood through Fertility Technologies.

From 2020 to 2023, I was a Visiting Scholar at the University of Montreal's School of Public Health, hosted by Prof. Vardit Ravitsky, deepening my comparative research on reproductive ethics and health policy across different legal and cultural contexts.

Beyond academia, I serve as an Ethical Consultant at Carmel Medical Center and as a member of the Ethics Committee at Maccabi Health Maintenance Organization, translating scholarly bioethics into real-world clinical and institutional practice.

My research has been presented at leading international venues including the World Congress of Bioethics, the Oxford–Mount Sinai Consortium on Bioethics, and the University of Zurich Institute for Biomedical Ethics.

Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of bioethics, health law, and science & technology studies, with a focus on how emerging medical technologies challenge existing ethical and policy frameworks.

🧬

Reproductive Ethics

Ethical, legal, and social dimensions of IVF, surrogacy, gamete donation, and fertility preservation. The right to parenthood as a fundamental right.

🤖

AI & Big Data in Medicine

Implications of artificial intelligence and big data in clinical practice, with a focus on reproductive medicine, privacy, and algorithmic accountability.

🏥

Health Policy & Systems

Comparative health policy, Israeli and global health systems, public funding of fertility treatments, and healthcare priorities in crises.

⚖️

Bioethics & Medical Law

Patient rights, autonomy, paternalism in clinical settings, ethical consulting in hospitals, and positive ethics as a management tool.

🦠

Public Health Ethics

Ethical dilemmas in pandemic response, government tracing, vaccination policy, and individual rights versus public health imperatives.

🌍

Early-Life Ethics

Ethical issues arising from prenatal diagnostics, genomic technologies in pregnancy, and beginning-of-life decisions across cultures and legal systems.
